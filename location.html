Directions & Travel

How to Reach Our Studio

Clear directions for local clients in Manchar and visitors arriving from neighboring towns.

From Manchar S.T. Bus Stand

Just a 2-minute walk (approx. 150 meters) from the main bus depot exit towards Patilwada into Shet Ambalal Trust Complex (Shop B-5).

From Narayangaon & Junnar

Approximately 15–20 minutes drive south via the Pune–Nashik Highway (NH 60). Take the Manchar town center entry towards Patilwada.

From Khed & Rajgurunagar

Around 25–30 minutes drive north on NH 60. Ample parking available near the complex for bridal consultation visits.

From Ghodegaon & Ambegaon Taluka

Direct connection via Ghodegaon–Manchar Road (approx. 15 minutes) terminating right near the S.T. Bus Stand junction.
