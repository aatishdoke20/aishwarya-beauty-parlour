Keratin treatment and hair smoothening are both salon services that may be considered by people who want more manageable hair, but they should not be treated as identical treatments. The product formula, heat process, starting hair condition and finish you want all influence the choice. If you are comparing a keratin treatment in Manchar with hair smoothening, begin by asking the stylist to explain the exact product, expected finish, process and aftercare before you book. Use the Services page to [compare the hair services currently offered at the salon](/services.html#hair-treatments).

Aishwarya Beauty Parlour’s current service menu lists Keratin Protein Treatment, Hair Smoothening, Hair Botox Treatment and Hair Straightening as separate services. Their names should not be used interchangeably. A consultation is the right place to understand which option, if any, suits your hair and expectations. If you are only looking for shaping or seasonal conditioning rather than a chemical service, read our [guide to choosing a haircut or hair spa](/blog/ladies-haircut-hair-spa-manchar/).

## Keratin treatment: what should you clarify?

The salon lists a Keratin Protein Treatment with an approximate service duration of 2-3 hours. Before confirming, ask what the treatment is designed to change: frizz, feel, styling time, volume or the overall finish. Ask how the product is applied, whether heat is used, what the aftercare involves and how the result may change as your hair grows.

“Keratin” is not a complete description of a formula. Different products can contain different ingredients and use different processes. Ask to see the product name and ingredient information rather than assuming that every keratin-labelled service is the same or automatically chemical-free.

## Hair smoothening: how is it different?

Hair smoothening is separately listed on the service menu, with an approximate duration of 2.5-3.5 hours. It is described as a texture-managing service intended to make hair easier to handle while retaining some movement. Ask the stylist what finish the specific product is expected to create on your hair: reduced bulk, softer movement, straighter appearance or another result. The answer should be based on your hair’s starting texture and the treatment formula, not a universal promise.

## What about hair botox and straightening?

Hair Botox Treatment and Hair Straightening are also separate entries in the salon’s menu. Do not assume that hair botox is a medical treatment or that it repairs split ends permanently. Ask which product is used, what it is intended to do, whether heat or chemical processing is involved, and how long the result typically lasts on hair similar to yours. Straightening or rebonding is a different decision again, especially if you want a much straighter result.

## Ask about ingredients, heat and ventilation

Some hair-smoothing products can release formaldehyde when heated. The U.S. Food and Drug Administration advises consumers to ask salon professionals about product ingredients and safety information; it identifies formaldehyde, formalin and methylene glycol as names to look for. This is general product-safety guidance, not a claim about the specific products used at Aishwarya Beauty Parlour. Before any smoothing service, ask for the exact product name, ingredient information, directions and safety precautions, and whether the salon has appropriate ventilation for the process.

Do not rely only on labels such as “organic”, “natural”, “protein”, “formaldehyde-free” or “damage-free”. Ask for the product information that supports the description. If you have asthma, sensitivity to fumes, scalp irritation, a history of allergic reactions or recently coloured/chemically treated hair, explain this before the stylist recommends a service.

## A consultation checklist before booking

- What exact product and treatment process will be used?
- Is heat applied, and what ventilation and precautions are in place?
- What finish should I realistically expect on my current hair texture?
- How will the result change with washing, regrowth and routine styling?
- What aftercare products or restrictions are recommended, and are they optional or required?
- What is the total price, approximate appointment time and any additional charge?
