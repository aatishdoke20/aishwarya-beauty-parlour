About Aishwarya Beauty Parlour —
Trusted Ladies Salon in Manchar

"Experience in beauty isn’t just measured in years — it is proven in hands-on precision, understanding individual skin tones, and passing authentic craft to the next generation."

Aishwarya Beauty Parlour (ऐश्वर्य ब्युटी पार्लर मंचर) is led by founder Varsha Doke in Patilwada, Manchar. With around two decades of hands-on salon experience, our studio is dedicated to providing authentic ladies beauty services, precision haircuts, relaxing facials, and personalized bridal styling in a welcoming, hygienic atmosphere.

Conveniently situated near the S.T. Bus Stand in Patilwada, our parlour regularly serves clients traveling from Manchar town and neighboring feeder villages across the Ambegaon taluka — including Kalamb, Pimpalgaon, Landewadi, and Ghodegaon.

Over the past 20 years, Varsha Doke has served more than 10,000+ satisfied clients and personally trained approximately 400+ aspiring beauty professionals, sharing foundational salon craftsmanship, brush mastery, and attentive client care.
