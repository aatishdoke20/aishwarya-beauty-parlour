Before booking, tell the beauty professional about known sensitivities, allergies, recent skin treatments and any products or procedures that have caused a reaction. A short consultation helps you understand what the service includes and whether it is appropriate for your skin on that day.

Start with what you want from the appointment

People often use the word “facial” to describe several different salon experiences. Some want a relaxing appointment and basic cleansing; others are looking for a more hydrated feel or a simple refresh before an occasion. Begin by explaining your goal in everyday language. You do not need to identify your skin type perfectly before you arrive.

If your skin often feels dry or tight, explain when that happens and which products you currently use. If your skin tends to feel oily or congested, describe the areas where you notice it. If it is reactive, easily irritated or currently uncomfortable, say so before any product is applied. These details are more useful than choosing a treatment only by name.

A salon facial is a cosmetic care service. It should not be presented as a treatment for persistent acne, eczema, rosacea, infection, unexplained rashes or other medical skin conditions. If you have an active skin problem, ongoing irritation or a recent procedure, ask a qualified dermatologist whether a facial is appropriate before booking.

Questions to ask before you begin

  • Which steps and products are included in today’s facial?
  • Can the service be adjusted for my known sensitivities or allergies?
  • Is steam or exfoliation part of the plan, and can either be skipped if my skin is reactive?
  • Is a patch test or a different approach advisable for a product I have not used before?
  • How long should I allow for the visit, and what is the current price?

How to prepare for a salon facial

Arrive with enough time to explain your skin concerns and your usual routine. Mention recent waxing, peels, prescription products, strong exfoliants or other treatments rather than assuming they are irrelevant. Do not hide a previous reaction to a skincare product. If you are attending before a wedding, festival or important photograph, read our Maharashtrian bridal makeup preparation guide to plan your timing well in advance; allow room to discuss timing and any possible reaction with the professional.

After the appointment, follow the aftercare instructions given for the products actually used. If you notice unexpected burning, swelling, persistent redness or another concerning reaction, contact a qualified healthcare professional rather than trying to treat it with another salon product.
